About

Seasonal Crop Advisor

Seasonal Crop Advisor is a web tool designed to help farmers, students, and agriculture enthusiasts identify crops suited to their soil and growing season. The system uses soil type, soil pH, nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, and season as inputs for crop prediction.
